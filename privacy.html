Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data on the Curemos platform.

Effective Date: June 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy applies to all users of the Curemos platform and describes our practices regarding personal and professional data. Curemos is built exclusively for licensed medical professionals and handles all data with clinical-grade confidentiality.

1. Introduction

Curemos ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Curemos platform. By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Who We Are

Curemos is a doctor-to-doctor medical consultation platform designed exclusively for licensed medical professionals. We are not a patient-facing platform and do not provide direct medical services to patients.

3. Information We Collect

a. Registration Information

  • Full name and professional title
  • Medical registration number and licensing details
  • Specialization and institution affiliation
  • Contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Profile photograph (optional)

b. Usage Data

  • Consultation logs (date, time, duration)
  • Communication metadata and related activity information
  • Device information and IP address
  • App usage analytics

c. Communication Content

  • Text messages exchanged during consultations
  • Audio and video recordings where explicitly enabled
  • Shared clinical notes, images, or documents

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity and medical credentials
  • To facilitate doctor-to-doctor consultations
  • To improve platform performance and user experience
  • To send important service notifications and updates
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity

5. Patient Data and Confidentiality

Curemos does not collect patient data directly. However, users may share patient-related information during consultations. Users are responsible for ensuring that appropriate patient consent has been obtained before sharing identifiable health information.

We strongly advise against sharing directly identifiable patient information unless it is necessary. Any patient data shared through the platform receives the same security protections as other platform data.

6. Data Storage and Security

Data transmitted through Curemos is protected using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols. Data is stored on secure servers with restricted access, supported by technical and organizational measures designed to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

While reasonable precautions are taken, no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Curemos does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only when necessary, including:

  • With your explicit consent
  • With trusted service providers operating under confidentiality obligations
  • When required by law, court order, or regulatory authority
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Curemos or its users

8. Data Retention

Personal data may be retained while your account remains active or for periods required by applicable law. Consultation records may be retained where required by medical or regulatory requirements. Users may request deletion of their account and associated data subject to applicable legal obligations.

9. Your Rights

As a Curemos user, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of personal data subject to legal obligations
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

10. Cookies and Tracking

The Curemos platform may use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience and understand usage patterns. Cookie settings can be controlled through your device or browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain platform features.

11. Third-Party Services

Curemos may integrate third-party services for functionality such as video calling, analytics, or cloud storage. Such providers may have their own privacy policies. Users are encouraged to review those policies before using related services.

12. Children's Privacy

Curemos is intended solely for licensed medical professionals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we become aware that a minor has registered, we will take appropriate steps to delete the account and associated data.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Significant changes may be communicated through the App or registered email.
