Marketing Policy

How Curemos communicates with users and handles data for marketing purposes.

Effective Date: August 2026

This Marketing Policy explains how Curemos communicates with registered users for promotional and informational purposes, and how we use personal data in the context of marketing. Curemos is committed to transparent, consent-based communication and does not sell user data to third parties. This policy is intended to satisfy the requirements of the Apple App Store and Google Play Store for app publication.

1. Overview

This Marketing Policy describes how Curemos ("we", "our", or "the App") communicates with its users for marketing and promotional purposes, and how we handle user data in the context of marketing activities. This policy applies to all registered users of the Curemos platform and supplements our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

2. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to licensed medical professionals who have registered on the Curemos platform. Curemos is a doctor-to-doctor consultation platform and is not intended for use by patients or the general public. All marketing communications are directed exclusively at verified medical professionals.

3. Types of Communications We Send

a. Transactional & Service Communications

  • Account registration confirmations and onboarding guidance
  • Consultation request notifications and reminders
  • Security alerts and account activity notifications
  • Policy updates and changes to Terms or Privacy Policy
  • Technical support responses and service announcements

b. Marketing & Promotional Communications

  • New feature announcements and product updates
  • Educational content relevant to medical professionals
  • Platform usage tips and best practices
  • Invitations to participate in surveys or feedback programs
  • Information about partnerships with medical institutions or associations

4. How We Collect Marketing Consent

We collect your consent to receive marketing communications at the time of registration through an explicit opt-in checkbox. You will not receive promotional communications unless you have affirmatively opted in. Transactional and service-related communications are sent regardless of marketing preferences, as they are necessary for the operation of the platform.

5. Use of Personal Data in Marketing

We use the following data solely to personalise and improve the relevance of our communications:

  • Your name and professional title (for personalised salutations)
  • Your medical specialization (to send relevant clinical content)
  • Your geographic location (to send region-specific updates or regulatory information)
  • Your app usage patterns (to suggest features you may not have used)
  • Your communication preferences (to respect opt-out choices)

6. No Sale of Personal Data

Curemos does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or marketing agencies. We do not use your data for targeted advertising on external platforms (such as social media networks or search engines) without your explicit consent. Any third-party service providers involved in delivering our communications (e.g., email delivery services) are bound by strict data processing agreements and may not use your data for their own marketing purposes.

7. In-App Communications

Curemos may display informational banners, tooltips, or notifications within the application to inform you of new features, updates, or relevant professional content. These in-app messages are part of the platform experience and are not subject to marketing opt-out preferences. They do not involve sharing your data with third parties.

8. Push Notifications

If you have enabled push notifications on your device, Curemos may send you push notifications for both service-related and marketing purposes. You can manage push notification preferences at any time through your device's operating system settings or within the Curemos app settings. Disabling push notifications will not affect your ability to use the platform.

9. Email Marketing

Marketing emails are sent only to users who have opted in to receive them. Every marketing email includes a clearly visible unsubscribe link. Clicking the unsubscribe link will remove you from our marketing mailing list within 10 business days. Unsubscribing from marketing emails will not affect transactional or service communications.

10. How to Opt Out

You may withdraw your consent to receive marketing communications at any time through any of the following methods:

  • Clicking the "Unsubscribe" link in any marketing email
  • Updating your communication preferences in the Curemos app under Settings → Notifications
  • Contacting us directly at support@curemos.app with the subject line "Marketing Opt-Out"
  • Disabling push notifications through your device operating system settings

11. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website (curemos.com) may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors interact with our content and to measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns. We use this data in aggregate and anonymised form only. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or through the cookie consent banner displayed on our website. For full details, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

12. Third-Party Links and Endorsements

Marketing communications from Curemos may occasionally include links to third-party websites, publications, or resources relevant to medical professionals. Inclusion of such links does not constitute an endorsement of the third party. Curemos is not responsible for the content, privacy practices, or marketing policies of any third-party websites.

13. Compliance with Applicable Laws

Our marketing practices comply with applicable laws and regulations, including:

  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 (India) and associated rules
  • The Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations (TRAI)
  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable to users in the European Economic Area
  • The CAN-SPAM Act where applicable to users in the United States
  • Apple App Store and Google Play Store guidelines on user communications

14. Children and Minors

Curemos is intended exclusively for licensed medical professionals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly market to or collect data from minors. If we become aware that marketing communications have been sent to a minor, we will immediately remove them from all marketing lists and delete any associated data.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Marketing Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will notify registered users of material changes via email or in-app notification. Your continued use of the platform following such notification constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.

16. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Marketing Policy or our communications practices, please contact us at:

Email: support@curemos.app

Website: curemos.com/contact

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 5 business days.
